approve
What I read: all 8 revised stories (`aep plan artifact show`; revisions 3 to 8), `aep plan artifact graph` and `aep plan artifact validate` (prints "valid"). I walked 24 edges, including ones outside the set (story:no-test-holds-a-wall-clock-time, and story:message-id-per-sender on to story:post-does-not-tell-a-message-id-exists). There is no cycle. I traced 8 acceptance reads to a producer in the set (binds reads tls; wake reads reconnect; claude_code, metaharness and terminal read wake; stage-3 checks read tls, reconnect and wake), and a depends_on edge records all 8. Both round-1 findings are settled. The wake story now owns the one-wake guard (Open 2 A: lock beside the outbox, exit 5), and claude_code relies on it and writes no guard of its own. Reconnect's Open 1 B now says it reverses the depends_on edge.
What I could not establish: (1) The 2 new edges (reconnect to tls, binds to terminal) make a chain tls, reconnect, wake, terminal, binds. Each story's Scope gives the reason: they share crates/inbox-cli/src/client.rs, main.rs, reference.rs, generated/cli/inbox.json and the mod list of tests/main.rs. By my procedure, an edge written down for a shared file is a trade-off, not a finding. The other option is to split those surfaces: generated/cli/inbox.json is regenerated by `task cli-reference`, and each tests/main.rs change is one mod line. Splitting would remove the two edges whose acceptance reads nothing from the target. (2) If terminal Open 1 C is picked (the story moves to stage 4), binds' depends_on edge on story:the-terminal-adapter-shows-a-post points at a story that has left the epic. It would then need to point at story:inbox-wake-returns-on-a-post. Noted, not a finding. (3) Out of my lane, for parallel-safety: whether commands.rs, which wake, reconnect and terminal all edit, is covered by the same order.
